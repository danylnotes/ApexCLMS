Operations Excellence · OPX-210

Operational Excellence for Refinery Operations

Practical application of performance measurement, loss analysis, standardization, root cause analysis, and continuous improvement in refinery operations.
IntermediateBlended8 h estimated study8 CPD hours (configured value)Updated 19 Oct 2026

Complete Process Safety Management Fundamentals before enrolling.

About this course

Operational excellence is the disciplined pursuit of safe, reliable and efficient operation. This course translates the principles into the tools a refinery uses every day: KPI trees, loss accounting, standard work, structured problem solving and the daily management system.

The course uses worked examples from crude distillation, hydrotreating and utilities to show how loss analysis directs improvement effort to where it matters most.

Why it matters

Refinery margin is highly sensitive to availability, yield and energy efficiency. A structured view of losses converts operational data into prioritised improvement actions and sustains gains through standard work.

Who should take it

Shift supervisors, senior operators, process engineers and production planners. Recommended for those moving into supervisory roles.

Refinery OperationsProcess EngineeringUtilities & Energy

What you will learn

  1. Construct a KPI tree linking unit-level measures to site performance.
  2. Quantify availability, rate and quality losses using a loss accounting framework.
  3. Develop and audit standard work for a critical operating task.
  4. Apply a structured root cause analysis to an operational incident.
  5. Run an effective tiered daily performance meeting.

Expected outcomes

  • A completed loss tree for a selected unit.
  • A standard work document ready for field trial.
  • An RCA report with verified corrective actions.

Prerequisites

Process Safety Management Fundamentals (PSM-101) or equivalent.
