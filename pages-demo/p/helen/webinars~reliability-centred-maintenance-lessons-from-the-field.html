Reliability and Maintenance · WEB-031

Reliability-Centred Maintenance: Lessons from the Field

Case studies of RCM implementation on critical rotating equipment, including how failure data reshaped maintenance intervals.
◆UpcomingThu, 29 Oct 2026, 01:26 GMT+31 h 15 minTime zone: Asia/Baghdad

Overview

Case studies of RCM implementation on critical rotating equipment, including how failure data reshaped maintenance intervals. The session includes a moderated question-and-answer segment.

Learning objectives

  • Explain why RCM programmes stall after the analysis phase.
  • Use failure data to challenge legacy maintenance intervals.
  • Identify hidden failures in protective devices.
