H₂S Awareness and Respiratory Protection

Properties and hazards of hydrogen sulphide, detection, alarm response, respiratory protection selection and rescue principles for sour service areas.
FoundationSelf-paced online2 h estimated study2 CPD hours (configured value)Updated 19 Oct 2026

About this course

Properties and hazards of hydrogen sulphide, detection, alarm response, respiratory protection selection and rescue principles for sour service areas. The course combines concise explanation with site-relevant examples and closes with a knowledge assessment.

Why it matters

Builds capability in h₂s awareness and respiratory protection directly relevant to safe and reliable operations.

Who should take it

Personnel in the listed departments whose role requires this knowledge.

Refinery OperationsMaintenance & ReliabilityProcess Safety & HSELaboratory & QualityUpstream OperationsUtilities & Energy

What you will learn

  1. Explain the key principles of properties and health effects of h₂s.
  2. Explain the key principles of detection and alarm response.
  3. Explain the key principles of respiratory protection and rescue.

Expected outcomes

  • Ability to apply the course principles in day-to-day work.
